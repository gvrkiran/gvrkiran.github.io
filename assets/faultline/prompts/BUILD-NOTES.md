# FAULTLINE — production blueprint

## Concept

**FAULTLINE: The Observatory at the End of Certainty** is theme 28 of the academic
site. It is one continuous forward pursuit through the same Rutgers-inspired
observatory while a single meteor impact destabilizes the building and the laws of
gravity. The event escalates causally across the existing pages — About, Projects,
Publications, Teaching, and Other — and resolves at dawn as the fragments reassemble.

The page names stay literal. The catastrophe lives in the world and motion, never in
the navigation or page copy.

## Journey

1. **About — The omen.** A calm brass-and-brick observatory. A meteor appears beyond
   the dome and strikes behind the campus. The camera follows the shockwave toward the
   Projects doorway.
2. **Projects — The signal storm.** The shockwave crosses a computational lab. Signal
   filaments overload, glass lifts, and masonry falls while the camera keeps advancing.
3. **Publications — The archive fault.** The floor tears into a glowing volcanic
   fissure. Papers and index cards spiral upward instead of burning, forming a tunnel
   through the archive.
4. **Teaching — The impossible classroom.** The tunnel opens into a Rutgers cloister
   during an earthquake and mudslide. Desks, chalk marks, and broken arches suspend in
   midair and assemble into a bridge.
5. **Other — Gravity gives way.** The bridge exits into open sky. Campus fragments,
   books, tools, and observatory machinery orbit a quiet center; the orbit reverses and
   the world rebuilds at dawn.

## Motion and continuity contract

- Architecture A: five sequential legs, no connectors and no cuts.
- Leg 1 starts from `00-opening-anchor.png`.
- Every later leg starts from the **actual last frame** of the previous rendered leg.
- Every leg begins by continuing the same slow forward glide and spends its final
  second settling back into that same glide.
- The camera always moves forward. Disaster motion may cross, rise, fall, or reverse,
  but the camera never backs up or teleports.
- Keep the central path readable through every event so the composition still works
  under the left-side copy panel.
- No people, bodies, injury, fire consuming the building, text, logos, captions, or
  hard scene changes in generated media.

## Art direction

Photoreal architectural fantasy: Rutgers-inspired red brick, warm ivory limestone,
aged brass, dark walnut, midnight navy, restrained scarlet, electric cyan signal
trails, volcanic amber, airborne dust, cinematic volumetric light. The building must
remain recognizable as one place even as physics fails.

## Production scope and budget

- Desktop: native 16:9 chain.
- Mobile: deliberate poster-based fallback; no landscape video cropped behind copy.
- Previz: five 5-second Seedance 2.0 clips at 480p = 75 Higgsfield credits.
- Final: five 6-second Seedance 2.0 clips at 1080p = 270 credits.
- Planned total: 345 credits, leaving approximately 81.52 credits for one 1080p reroll
  from the verified balance of 426.52 credits.
- Opening anchor: built-in GPT image generation, not Higgsfield credits.

No paid generation begins until this budget is explicitly approved.
